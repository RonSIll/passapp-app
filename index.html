PassApp

Add PassApp to your Home Screen first. Safari erases a website's data after a week without a visit, and that data includes your key.

  1. Tap Share at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open PassApp from its new icon.

Step 1 of 3

Your recovery key

This key adds PassApp to a new phone or PC. Lose every device without it, and your passwords are gone for good.

Write it on paper, or print it from your PC. Don't save it as a PDF or keep it on your phone. Anyone with this key and access to your GitHub account can read every password.

Step 2 of 3

Check your copy

Type the last four characters of your recovery key.

Connect to GitHub

PassApp keeps your scrambled passwords in your private passapp-data repo. This device needs its own token to reach it.

Lost a device?

Replacing the vault key re-encrypts every password and gives you a new recovery key. Your other devices will each need that new key once.

Then do two more things. On GitHub, delete the lost device's token, named PassApp iPhone or PassApp PC. And if anyone may have opened the lost device, change those passwords at each website, since a lost device keeps what it already had.
